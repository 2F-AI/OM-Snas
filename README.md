OM Sans

OM Sans is our custom typeface designed specifically for the MuffinLM web interface. It’s a modern, organic-looking font crafted to provide a cohesive and balanced visual experience for both digital and print.

What is OM Sans?

OM Sans is an open-source font created with a focus on harmony and consistency. Every letter is designed to reflect a smooth, organic aesthetic with rounded shapes and uniform proportions, making the font feel warm, accessible, and modern. Each character maintains a balance of clean, geometric lines with gentle curves, resulting in a harmonious design that’s easy to read and visually appealing.

What Do We Want From the Font?

OM Sans was developed with the goal of providing an open-source, versatile typeface that can be used freely in any project. Whether you're working on a website, branding, or print materials, OM Sans delivers a modern and friendly look. The font’s consistent proportions and organic forms make it suitable for a wide range of applications.

By releasing OM Sans as open-source, we hope to make a high-quality typeface available to the design community. We want to encourage creativity and innovation, allowing designers and developers to use OM Sans freely in their projects while contributing to its ongoing development and refinement.

Key Features

Organic Consistency: Every character, from "A" to "Z" and everything in between, follows the same organic design language with rounded corners and smooth curves.

Balanced Proportions: Key elements like crossbars (in letters such as "f" and "t") are aligned at the same height, providing a balanced visual structure across the font.

Geometric with a Natural Touch: The font combines clean, geometric shapes with subtle organic curves, making it versatile for both modern and creative uses.

Open Source: OM Sans is freely available for personal and commercial projects, with the goal of providing an accessible and flexible font for everyone.
